Copy on creative

No melatonin · No sedatives · No next-day grogginess 6 research-backed ingredients that work WITH your body

Visual treatment

Split between dark navy (problem/old way) and warm green (solution/Mellow). Mellow product centered. 'NO' strikethrough list on left vs 'YES' list on right.

Why this keeps running

Cross-brand

What every long-running magnesium ad shares

Hook types that keep running
  • Reframe hook: 'You don't have X problem, you have Y problem'
  • Authority hook: ingredient reveals with ® brand names
  • Contrarian hook: 'Your current supplement/approach doesn't work because...'
  • Result hook: morning-outcome framing (wake up restored/refreshed/without ache)
Copy elements in every long-runner
  • Melatonin-free or no-dependency claim (100% of long runners)
  • Specific ingredient forms, not just 'magnesium' (100% of long runners)
  • Morning outcome, not just sleep onset (80% of long runners)
  • Third-party validation signal (lab test / ® ingredient / clinical study) (90%)
  • Single ritual instruction (1 scoop / 2 tablets / mix with water) (70%)
Formats, ranked by longevity
  • Ingredient-reveal video (longest running — 300-420 days across brands)
  • Dark background badge static (second longest — 150-200 days)
  • Problem-reframe UGC/voiceover (60-120 days)
  • Comparison/split-screen static (40-80 days)
Calm > Urgent. Every ad running 100+ days is calm in its presentation. High-energy urgency ads burn out in 30-45 days. The sleep category demands that the AD ITSELF signals calm — the product promise must be felt in the creative execution.
The opening

Where Cosmix wins

No India-first brand has run a saffron-led magnesium ad. Saffron as a sleep botanical is clinically studied but culturally unassociated with supplements in Indian DTC. Cosmix is 'formulated by herbalists' — saffron is their unique right-to-play that neither Ace Blend nor Supply6 owns.

Hook types to test
  1. Reframe: 'Your body doesn't want melatonin. It wants magnesium and saffron.'
  2. Education: 'Here's why Indians specifically are more magnesium deficient than global averages.'
  3. Demonstration: Show the three forms dissolving — Bisglycinate (fast), Malate (sustained), Citrate (cellular) — as a visual proof of the triple complex
Formats to test
  1. Slow UGC-style video (match Supply6's calm energy, not Ace Blend's science tone)
  2. Dark background product badge static with 'Triple Mg + Saffron' as the headline claim
  3. Ingredient reveal video in Cosmix's earthy visual language (warm greens + saffron gold)
